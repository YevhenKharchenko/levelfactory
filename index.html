How to Play

Take your time with this one - rushing will just get you stuck.

  • Study the maze layout to find the safest route

  • Swipe or tap to guide snakes through paths and around obstacles

  • Avoid dead ends and plan ahead — one wrong move may trap your snake

  • Solve each puzzle with logic, not reflexes

  • Progress through hundreds of levels with increasing challenge

Features & Benefits

You'll definitely get better at thinking ahead, plus it's just fun to play.

  • Hundreds of handcrafted maze levels with clever challenges

  • Intuitive controls make navigation easy for all ages
